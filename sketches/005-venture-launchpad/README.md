## Variant: Venture Launchpad

### Design stance
"Better Polsia" — Polsia's *start a company tonight* energy, but every step runs on auditable agentic crypto rails, and Vic (chief of staff chat) walks you through the launch live.

### Polsia vs Shipyard
| | Polsia | Shipyard OS |
|---|---|---|
| Pitch | AI runs your company while you sleep | Launch & run ventures on agentic crypto rails |
| Visibility | Black box ("trust the agents") | Thinking traces, tool chips, task rows |
| Money | Subscriptions, no crypto | Per-venture treasury wallets, x402 micro-payments, agent wages, Buoy marketplace |
| Launch flow | "Get Started" | Chat a one-liner → agent scaffolds venture, derives wallet, wires x402, deploys |

### Key choices
- Layout: Ventures portfolio deck (revenue sparklines, per-venture treasuries) + Vic launch chat
- New primitives: launch checklist with rail-staged states (◈), treasury card (USDC in/out), spend-policy approval card, "List on Buoy" recommendation, `◈ pay` composer affordance
- Sidebar gains a **Rails** section: Treasury / x402 Payments / Buoy, with the treasury wallet pinned at bottom

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: shows the crypto rails as product, not bolt-on; every dollar is traceable
- Weak at: assumes USDC/x402 fluency — onboarding copy must teach gently

### Best for
Founders who want Polsia's promise but with receipts: watch the agents, watch the wallet.
